FAQ

How do I book a trip through your website? Browse destinations or packages, select your dates and preferences, and complete checkout. You'll get a confirmation email right after booking.
What is your cancellation and refund policy? Cancellation terms vary by package and how far in advance you cancel. Refunds are typically processed within 7 to 14 business days, depending on your bank and payment method.
What's included in the package price? Most packages include accommodation, transport, and specified meals or activities. Exact inclusions and exclusions are listed on each package's details page.
Do I need a visa or travel insurance for my destination? Visa requirements depend on your nationality and destination — we recommend checking the official embassy website. Travel insurance may be optional or bundled depending on the package; check the package details or contact our team for guidance.
Is support available if something goes wrong during my trip? Yes, our support team is available 24/7 to assist with emergencies, flight delays, or any other issues that arise during your travel.
